# Stuff

Stuff is an unprocessed captured item. It can be an idea, reminder, note, reference, commitment, file-backed asset, or anything that still needs clarification.

Stuff starts in the inbox. It should not be treated as a commitment until it is processed into a clearer GTD element.

Use [[Inbox]] to capture, inspect, edit, process, and delete stuff.

Stuff can include supporting [[Body Content]] when the captured material needs notes, links, or attached files.

## States

### Captured

Captured stuff is still unprocessed and belongs in the inbox until it is clarified.

### Deleted

Deleted stuff is captured inbox material that was removed before or after clarification.

Deleted stuff exists as a recoverable workflow state. Restore it when it was deleted by mistake.
